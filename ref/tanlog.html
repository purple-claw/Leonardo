Why This Matters: The Power of $\mathbf{A}^{100}$

In AI, we often apply a transformation over and over (like in deep neural networks or predicting future states). Multiplying a matrix by itself 100 times is incredibly slow. But with diagonalization, it becomes trivial.

$k=1$ $k=50$ $k=100$

Notice: As $k$ increases, the normal way requires massive matrix multiplication. The diagonal way only requires raising two simple numbers to the power of $k$. Instant computation!
